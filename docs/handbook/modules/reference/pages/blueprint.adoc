= Service Blueprint
:description: Layout of Service Blueprints and Journey Maps: cards, stacking, footer, labels.

A `blueprint(...)` model renders as a grid: phases are columns, the four Shostack layers are rows. This page
describes how a step card and a band are laid out and how to switch the language of the texts.

[kuml, blueprint-footer, svg]
----
blueprint(name = "Membership") {
    val staff = actor(name = "Member office", role = ActorRole.STAFF)
    val crm = actor(name = "CRM", role = ActorRole.SYSTEM)
    val web = channel(name = "Web", kind = ChannelKind.WEB)
    val form = touchpoint(name = "Form", channel = web)
    phase(name = "Application") {
        customer(name = "Fills in the application", sentiment = Sentiment.NEUTRAL, touchpoints = listOf(form))
        frontstage(name = "Confirms receipt", actor = staff, touchpoints = listOf(form))
        backstage(name = "Checks the data", actor = staff)
        backstage(name = "Creates the member record", actor = crm)
    }
    blueprintDiagram(name = "Membership", emotionCurve = false)
}
----

== The step card

* The title uses the full card width (168 px) and wraps at word boundaries. A word that is longer than a line
  is cut with a hyphen. A title is limited to 300 characters and 12 lines; an ellipsis marks the cut.
* A pain point and an opportunity share one marker row above the footer (pain dot and caption left, opportunity
  dot right). The caption is one line; the tooltip has the full text.
* A step with an actor and/or touchpoints has a *footer* row: the actor's role icon and name left, the touchpoints
  right. A long actor name wraps to at most three lines (then it ends in an ellipsis; the tooltip names role and
  full name) and the card grows by the extra lines; a one-line name leaves the card height unchanged.
* A touchpoint is a badge in the symbol shape of the touchpoint (circle, diamond, square, hexagon) with its legend
  number. Badges are 20 px apart. If they do not fit beside at least 60 px of actor name, the last ones collapse
  into one `+n` chip. The legend below the diagram lists number, symbol, channel glyph and name.
* The card height follows the content (title lines, marker row, footer), at least 80 px. The cards of one layer
  fill the band: a card with a short title in a tall band is stretched to the same height as its neighbours.

== Bands and stacking

* Every layer band is as tall as the tallest cell of that layer, at least 112 px.
* Several steps of the same phase and layer are *stacked* in declaration order, 14 px apart. The band grows with
  the stack. More than 12 cards in one cell are reported as a warning by the constraint check.
* Connections run from card centre to card centre. A note anchor ends at the card it belongs to.

== Labels

The swimlane headers, the three line captions and the tooltip prefixes are English by default. Pass `labels` to
`blueprintDiagram` or `journeyDiagram` to change them; nothing depends on the JVM locale.

[source,kotlin]
----
blueprintDiagram(name = "Mitgliedschaft", labels = BlueprintLabels.GERMAN)
----

[cols="2,2,2"]
|===
| Field | English (default) | `BlueprintLabels.GERMAN`

| `customerActions` | Customer Actions | Kundenaktionen
| `frontstage` | Frontstage | Frontstage
| `backstage` | Backstage | Backstage
| `supportProcesses` | Support Processes | Unterstützende Prozesse
| `lineOfInteraction` | Line of Interaction | Interaktionslinie
| `lineOfVisibility` | Line of Visibility | Sichtbarkeitslinie
| `lineOfInternalInteraction` | Line of Internal Interaction | Linie der internen Interaktion
| `painPrefix` | Pain: | Schmerzpunkt:
| `opportunityPrefix` | Chance: | Chance:
|===

Other languages: `BlueprintLabels(customerActions = "...", ...)`. A swimlane header wraps inside the 132 px label
column; headers and captions are limited to 60 characters.

== Limits

* The text width is estimated with an average character width, so a very wide font can still reach the card edge.
* The LaTeX renderer uses the labels but has no footer: the actor is a `[ROLE]` suffix of the title.
